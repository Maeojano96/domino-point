Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 10, 2026

1 Introduction

Domino Point ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our score tracking application.

2 Information We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Player names you enter (stored locally on your device)
  • Game scores and history (stored locally)
  • Language preference
  • Theme settings

Automatically Collected:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage statistics
  • Crash reports
  • Advertising ID (for AdMob ads)

3 How We Use Your Information

  • To provide score tracking functionality
  • To save your game progress locally
  • To display personalized advertisements via Google AdMob
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

4 Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising identifier
  • IP address
  • App interaction data

For more info: Google Privacy Policy

5 Data Storage

All game data (scores, player names, history) is stored locally on your device using LocalStorage. We do not store this data on our servers. This app is hosted on Netlify.

6 Your Rights

You can:

  • Clear all data via Settings → Clear All Games
  • Disable ads by purchasing premium (if available)
  • Request data deletion by contacting us

7 Children Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8 Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with a new date.

9 Contact

For questions about this app, please contact the app developer directly.

10 Consent

By using Domino Point, you consent to this Privacy Policy and our data practices.

Questions?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out.
