FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a new game? ▾

Open the app, select your game mode (2 Players, 3 Players, 4 Players, or 2v2 Teams), enter player names, set your target score, and tap "🚀 START GAME".

Can I save my game progress? ▾

Yes! During gameplay, tap the "💾 Save" button to save your current game. You can load saved games from the main screen under "💾 Saved Games".

How do I change the language? ▾

Go to ⚙️ Settings → 🌐 Language. Domino Point supports 12 languages including English, Arabic, Kurdish, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Turkish, and Hindi.

Can I customize the score target? ▾

Yes! On the setup screen, tap "🎯 Target Score" and choose from preset values (100, 120, 150, 200, 500) or enter a custom target up to 1000 points.

How do team modes work? ▾

Select "🤝 2v2 Teams" mode. Players 1 & 2 form Team A, Players 3 & 4 form Team B. Scores are combined per team, and the team reaching the target score first wins!

Is my data stored online? ▾

No. All game data is stored locally on your device using LocalStorage. We do not store any data on our servers. Your privacy is important to us.

How do I clear all my data? ▾

Go to ⚙️ Settings → 🗑️ Data → "🗑️ Clear All Games". This will delete all saved games and history from your device.

Troubleshooting

🔋 App not loading

  • Refresh the page (pull down on mobile)
  • Clear browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
  • Check your internet connection

📱 Scores not saving

  • Ensure LocalStorage is enabled in your browser
  • Don't use private/incognito mode (data won't persist)
  • Check if your device has storage space available
  • Try manually saving with the "💾 Save" button

🎨 Display issues

  • Update your browser to the latest version
  • Disable dark mode extensions that might conflict
  • Zoom out if the layout looks broken (Ctrl+0 or Cmd+0)
  • Try rotating your device if on mobile

🔊 Sound not working

  • Check if your device is on silent mode
  • Ensure volume is turned up
  • Toggle sound in ⚙️ Settings → 🔊 Sound
  • Some browsers block audio until user interaction
